# CAIOS Skill: Stop pitching. Start diagnosing.

## When to use it

Any high-stakes call where you're the one who has to close it, discovery calls, demos, anything where it's just you and the person who can say yes.

## Why it's helpful

I run every close on one rule: it's a diagnosis, not a pitch. I open the meeting by framing it like a doctor's visit, I ask questions, they answer, then they decide if they want the prescription. Most of the sale gets made in that discovery conversation, not when you finally say a number.

The naive version, jumping straight to explaining your product, loses people. Nobody buys because you're smart. They buy the transformation. Your product is the invisible plumbing underneath it.

The other half: never name a price before the prospect has told you what the problem is actually costing them. If it's costing them 25k a year and you're pricing at 3k, that's an obvious yes. Skip that step and you're just guessing at a number and hoping it lands.

## How to make it your own

- Write your own doctor-line frame, word for word, so it comes out natural instead of rehearsed.
- Build your own discovery openers around the gaps you actually solve, not generic ones.
- Fix your own 1-2 most common objections and the exact line you use to isolate money from want. Most people conflate "can't afford it" with "don't actually want it," and those need different responses.
- Have your AI build you a one-page battle card before every call: the frame, your top discovery questions, the anchor, the pitch in 3 steps max, the price and your floor logic. Glance at it 5 minutes before. Don't read from it live.

## Try it this week

On your next call, before you explain anything about what you do, ask one question: "what have you already tried, and what's different now that you'd spend money on this?" That single question does more selling than your entire pitch deck.
