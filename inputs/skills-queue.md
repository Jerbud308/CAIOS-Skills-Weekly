# Skills Queue

Ordered list. The routine picks the first slug not yet covered in `outputs/`.
Each entry: slug, source SKILL.md path, one-line description, and (where the
skill itself is business/tool-specific) the underlying transferable pattern
to write the post around.

Update this file by hand when you want to add, remove, or reorder a skill.
Never let the routine invent an entry that isn't listed here.

---

1. **sales-playbook**, `~/.claude/skills/sales-playbook/SKILL.md`
   Consultative B2B sales and closing methodology, including building an
   on-brand pitch deck. Pattern: a repeatable discovery-to-close structure,
   not a script.

2. **insight-catcher**, `~/.claude/skills/insight-catcher/SKILL.md`
   Notices genuinely shareworthy ideas as they surface in a working session
   and saves them to a running content folder. Pattern: a content radar so
   good ideas survive past the moment you had them.

3. **think-deeper**, `~/.claude/skills/think-deeper/SKILL.md`
   A structured decision protocol using 6 mental models to stress-test a
   choice before committing. Pattern: a repeatable checklist for hard
   decisions instead of gut-call-and-hope.

4. **converge-diverge**, `~/.claude/skills/converge-diverge/SKILL.md`
   Runs 10 independent perspectives on a decision in parallel, then
   synthesizes. Pattern: get the benefit of a 10-person advisory panel
   without needing 10 people.

5. **pulse-check**, `~/.claude/skills/pulse-check/SKILL.md`
   A month-to-date reflection ritual: what's working, what's been ignored,
   a ranked next-10. Pattern: a recurring "state of the business" review
   that pulls from real numbers, not vibes.

6. **launch-strategy**, `~/.claude/skills/launch-strategy/SKILL.md`
   Plans and executes a product/feature/campaign launch end to end.
   Pattern: a launch checklist that doesn't skip the boring parts.

7. **task-audit**, `~/.claude/skills/task-audit/SKILL.md`
   Maps out where your time and tasks are actually going. Pattern: an
   honest audit of your own bandwidth before you promise anyone anything.

8. **brainstorm**, `~/.claude/skills/brainstorm/SKILL.md`
   Crystallizes a fuzzy idea and stress-tests assumptions before it becomes
   a plan. Pattern: separate "what am I actually trying to build" from
   "how do I build it."

9. **deep-research**, `~/.claude/skills/deep-research/SKILL.md`
    Multi-source research across web, video, community, and real-time
    discourse, synthesized into one brief. Pattern: how to actually go
    deep on a market or a prospect instead of one Google search.

10. **eli5**, `~/.claude/skills/eli5/SKILL.md`
    Re-explains anything, a decision, a technical concept, a plan, in
    plain language a non-expert can follow. Pattern: the client-facing
    translation layer every consultant needs.

11. **writing-style**, `~/.claude/skills/writing-style/SKILL.md`
    Enforces prose that sounds like a sharp human, not a language model,
    across copy, proposals, and reports. Pattern: a house style you can
    actually hold AI output to.

12. **claude-context-coach**, `~/.claude/skills/claude-context-coach/SKILL.md`
    Keeps an AI assistant's memory and instructions organized so it stops
    repeating the same mistakes. Pattern: give your AI a real memory
    system instead of re-explaining yourself every session.

13. **schedule**, `~/.claude/skills/schedule/SKILL.md`
    Sets up recurring AI-run tasks on a cron so they happen without being
    asked. Pattern: the difference between an assistant you prompt and one
    that has a standing job.

14. **churn-risk**, `~/.claude/skills/churn-risk/SKILL.md`
    Scores active customers on churn risk using billing and usage signals,
    surfaces at-risk accounts before they cancel. Pattern: an early-warning
    system built from data you already have, not a guess.

15. **user360**, `~/.claude/skills/user360/SKILL.md`
    Pulls one customer's full picture, billing, auth, product usage, into
    a single lookup instead of five browser tabs. Pattern: consolidate
    scattered customer data into one view before every important call.
